## Contract corrections

- Creation cardinality distinguishes exact from maximum, and option menus are
  exclusive. Candidate eligibility and resulting type addition/replacement are
  independent.
- Command channel, cost, timing, range, batching, order persistence, fallback,
  and initiative are independent. Source-silent costs and fallbacks are omitted.
- Live-state dimensions remain orthogonal: effect, presence, persistent link,
  control, attitude, form, and integrity. Each semantic profile licenses only
  its source-supported axes and values.
- Transitions carry complete preconditions and explicit changes/operations.
  Reachability validation rejects impossible selectors. Relative timers have
  anchors; Elemental/Fey concentration-break removal is anchored to the original
  cast, with explicit precedence over ordinary spell-end removal.
- Familiar and Steed physical absence is independent of their persistent link;
  terminal release/dismissal cannot route to same-identity restoration.
- Animate Objects includes its complete fixed construct overlay and source-owned
  attack/movement/sense procedure while object-shape classification remains
  model-adjudicated.
- Familiar sense sharing and touch delivery, Phantom Steed equipment tether,
  and Simulacrum full-cast eligibility, repair prerequisites, dispel behavior,
  and recast cleanup are typed.
- F2/F3/F9/F10 are declarations only. Action budgets, concentration execution,
  math, currency spending, timers, and live actors remain external owners.
