## Pack records changed?

Yes. Fourteen spell records now carry S1 typed `summoning` effects:
`spell:animate-dead`, `spell:animate-objects`, `spell:conjure-animals`,
`spell:conjure-celestial`, `spell:conjure-elemental`, `spell:conjure-fey`,
`spell:conjure-minor-elementals`, `spell:conjure-woodland-beings`,
`spell:create-undead`, `spell:find-familiar`, `spell:find-steed`,
`spell:giant-insect`, `spell:phantom-steed`, and `spell:simulacrum`. No
records were added or removed.

The new payloads model appearance shape, command economy, initiative/control
mode, lifecycle transitions, type changes, source requirements, dismissal,
statblock modifications, telepathy, sense sharing, spell delivery/sharing, and
repair costs where the SRD states them.

Review fixes also model deterministic clauses that were previously omitted:
`animate-dead` and `animate-objects` per-slot target scaling,
`create-undead` higher-slot ghast/wight/mummy alternatives, `phantom-steed`
normal and fast travel rates, and `giant-insect` acting on the caster's turn.

Re-review fixes also model the `animate-objects` zero-HP reversion trigger,
upcast reassert-control scaling/options for `animate-dead` and
`create-undead`, `find-familiar` permanent dismissal and 100-foot touch spell
delivery requirement, `find-steed` action dismissal and bond release, and
`simulacrum` acting on the caster's turn according to the caster's wishes.

Third-review fixes stop the shared control shape from forcing invented rules
and close source-defined gaps:

- `control`, and its `mode`/`defaultBehavior`/`initiative` fields, are now
  optional and emitted only where the SRD states them.
  `find-familiar`/`giant-insect` drop an invented no-command default behavior;
  `find-steed` keeps only its exclusive bond; `phantom-steed` carries no
  control block at all (it is a rideable mount, not a commanded creature).
- `find-familiar` now carries BOTH source-defined recast transitions:
  recast-while-active → adopt new form, and recast-after-0-HP →
  `familiar-reappears`. Recast entries carry a required `priorState`
  ('active' | 'gone'), and `find-steed`/`simulacrum` recasts were annotated to
  match.
- Creation eligibility is typed: `animate-dead` gains Medium/Small humanoid
  target eligibility, base count of one, and a different-corpse-per-creature
  gate; `create-undead` gains Medium/Small humanoid eligibility;
  `animate-objects` gains nonmagical / not-worn-or-carried / no-larger-than-Huge
  eligibility.
- The dismissal recall schema is now a discriminated pair
  (pocket-dimension ⟺ action recall with range; dismissed ⟺ recast recall),
  rejecting the previously accepted impossible cross-combinations.

Fourth-review fixes deepen the persistent-companion state machine and finish
two eligibility/dismissal gaps:

- `priorState` gained a third state: recast preconditions are now
  'active' | 'absent-recoverable', and actor-removal transitions carry an
  optional `resultingState` ('absent-recoverable' | 'terminated'). Find Steed's
  0-HP and action-dismissal disappearances are `absent-recoverable` while action
  bond release is `terminated`, so a post-release recast can no longer route to
  `same-steed-returns-restored`. Find Familiar's 0-HP disappearance is
  `absent-recoverable`; simulacrum's melt is `terminated`.
- `option-menu` gained `eligibleTypes` (candidate selection gate): Conjure
  Animals `['beast']` (coexisting with its post-summon fey treatment), Conjure
  Minor Elementals `['elemental']`, Conjure Woodland Beings `['fey']`.
- Phantom Steed now types its action-cost dismissal
  (`action-dismissal:effect-fades`, 1-minute fade) instead of leaving it as
  prose; Giant Insect's dismissal is scoped `per-target`, and its unsupported
  `commandEconomy: on-your-turn` was removed (the source clause is initiative,
  not command timing).
- Every emitted S1 control constant
  (`mode`/`defaultBehavior`/`initiative`/`commandEconomy`/`eligibleTypes`) now
  has a `requireClauses` guard for its exact source prose, closing the
  fail-closed gaps in the conjuration and Find Familiar projections.

## Importer changed?

Yes. Spell mechanics projection now emits a source-gated `summoning` effect
for each reviewed S1 spell. Each projection verifies reviewed source clause
shapes before emitting constants, so a name-preserving prose drift fails closed
instead of retaining stale summoning semantics.

The source gates include higher-slot scaling/options, travel rates,
creature-type choice, exclusive-instance recast clauses, and familiar
dismissal/recall clauses where those constants are emitted.

Re-review gates additionally cover persistent-companion dismissal/release
clauses and the Simulacrum control paragraph before emitting those constants.
