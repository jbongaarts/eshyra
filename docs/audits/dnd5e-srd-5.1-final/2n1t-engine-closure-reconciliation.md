## Why no category is `PREMATURE CLOSURE`

The full set does not support a premature-closure finding. For `.1`, `.2`,
`.3`, `.10`, `.13`, `b69j.13`, and `o9bd.18.7`, the bead's own acceptance was
about a different policy, interaction flow, importer scope, or cross-cutting
precondition and was met. For `.4`–`.9`, `.11`, and `.12`, the bead's own
primitive acceptance was met, but later clause-complete pack requirements were
not integrated into that primitive. For `.18.7.8`, the close acceptance was a
rule-classification/readiness mapping that is now stale relative to a broader
source inventory. The 2026-07-24 finding of engine-pending clauses therefore
blocks any global completeness claim, but it does not objectively invalidate
each historical close reason.

## Corrective ownership

The bootstrap ledger assigns qualified primitives to the open
`eshyra-o9bd.19.5.2` through `.11` family epics where an exact owner exists.
It records proposed new titles under `eshyra-olc5` for cross-family
spellbook-copy/asset-cost, containment/portal/card-pool, planar-return/deadline
clock, and retained-asset/provenance primitives. No old bead is reopened,
closed, or modified here. The authoritative ledger and its decomposition gate
remain the supervisor's follow-up work.
